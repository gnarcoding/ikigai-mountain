    Every lap circled the same center

    Look down on the mountain and the four faces turn back into four circles. The trail you walked is a spiral through all of them, over and over. No one reaches the middle by standing still on one side, and no one skips the hard faces. You go around until the circles overlap under your feet.

    The four-circle diagram is a modern Western take on the idea. In Japan, ikigai usually means something smaller and more everyday: whatever makes your days feel worth living.
