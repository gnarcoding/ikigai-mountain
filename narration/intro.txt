The Ikigai Mountain.

No one lands on the summit. You start at the foot of one side and circle all four, again and again, until the paths meet at the top.

This is the whole trail, stop by stop. Every stop is a place where real climbers get stuck. As you listen, the climber on the mountain walks with you.

The mountain has four faces, one for each circle of ikigai. The north face is what you love. The west face is what you're good at. The east face is what the world needs. And the south face is what you can be paid for.

Where two circles overlap, the ground rises into a ridge: Passion, Mission, Vocation and Profession. Where three overlap it rises again, and each of those ledges is missing exactly one circle. Where all four meet is the summit.

Wherever you start, you'll pass every stop on the mountain. Let's begin at the foot of the love side.
